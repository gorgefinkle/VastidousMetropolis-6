R14.15 DEV — One Creek Origin per City
Java target: 25
Paper API: 26.2.build.129-stable

R14.15 retains the R14.14 Bloomlands minimum province dimensions, matching fungal landmark picker seed, broad-core biome teleport validation, and R14.13 inland Karst/open landbridge/deep geology. It corrects the creek-origin uniqueness gap:
- Creek-origin schematics are fixed landmarks supplied by a separate FixedPlanner; they previously bypassed the normal LandmarkPlanner's per-city uniqueness decision.
- Each accepted creek-origin park is associated with the exact generated HighwayNetwork.City containing its park centre.
- All viable 64x64 host-tile candidates inside that city's actual box are enumerated and arbitrated by a deterministic unsigned site-rank. Exactly one creek origin can be accepted in that city, independent of tile generation/query order.
- Candidate origins outside a present highway-city box retain their previous behavior.
- QA verifies stable one-winner-per-city arbitration and city-box lookup at negative and positive world coordinates.

The package remains a DEV build pending automated validation and in-world testing.
