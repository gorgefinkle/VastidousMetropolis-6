R14.17 DEV — Branch Growth Timeout Shutdown Guard
Java target: 25
Paper API: 26.2.build.129-stable

R14.17 fixes a plugin-lifecycle defect exposed during server shutdown. Pending branch-growth readiness watchdogs can fire after Metropolis is disabled. Previously, an unpopulated parcel caused the callback to register another 200-tick task without checking plugin state, producing IllegalPluginAccessException during shutdown.

Timeout creation and timeout rescheduling now both require the plugin to remain enabled and the exact build accumulator to remain pending. A late callback after disable exits without scheduling another task. BranchGrowthDrain.enqueue() also treats late enqueue requests after shutdown as no-ops instead of recreating a ticker or throwing.

R14.17 retains the R14.16 per-column mushroom cap/stem clearance, R14.15 one-creek-origin-per-city arbitration, R14.14 Bloomlands province sizing and fungal landmark picker correction, and inherited Karst geology work.

QA/R14_17/run_growth_timeout_shutdown_policy_probe.sh checks active/pending, disabled/pending, active/retired, and disabled/retired states.

This is a shutdown-safety correction only. It does NOT claim to solve branch-growth queue throughput or memory retention; those remain separate generation-performance work.
