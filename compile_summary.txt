R14.16 DEV — Mushroom Cap / Stem Occlusion
Java target: 25
Paper API: 26.2.build.129-stable

R14.16 fixes a visible fungal landmark geometry defect. The giant mushroom trunk previously continued to a fixed height even when one of the cap profiles curved down through that height. In those variants the mushroom_stem blocks passed through the cap and appeared on the upper surface.

The geometry now derives the lowest cap voxel for each horizontal column after assembling the complete cap/crown (including branch caps). Trunk voxels are emitted only below that cap underside in the same column. This keeps the trunk connected to the underside while preventing it from occupying or emerging above the cap. The cap profile, species selection, footprint, and site/terrain ownership are unchanged.

QA/R14_16/run_mushroom_cap_stem_probe.sh exercises all six generated mushroom profiles over 96 deterministic seed/site combinations and asserts that no visible stem voxel reaches or crosses the cap in any shared vertical column.

R14.16 retains R14.15 one-creek-origin-per-city arbitration, R14.14 Bloomlands province sizing and production fungal landmark picker corrections, and the R14.13 inland Karst/open landbridge work.

The package remains a DEV build pending automated validation and in-world visual testing.
